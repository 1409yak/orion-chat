Liquid Glass

Turn on liquid glass (has impact on performance)

Glossy, light-catching glass panels with bright rims. Turn off to switch to the sleek, flat Minimalist Modern theme.

Liquid glass animations

Drifting light behind the glass, a soft glow that follows your cursor, springy buttons and a fluid pop-in for windows. Turn off to keep the glass look but make it still.

Optimisation

Performance mode

Removes all animations and transitions, and hides animated backgrounds. Best for older phones and low battery. Turns liquid glass off while active.

Turns off the frosted-glass blur behind panels — the single biggest GPU cost. Panels get a slightly darker tint instead.
